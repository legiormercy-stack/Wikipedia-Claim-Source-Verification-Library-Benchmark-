# Overview
These are the guidelines used to verify the claims in the dataset. 

## Guidelines
1. Identify the claim/proposition you want to verify.
2. Confirm the source link health or if it is inaccessible. (An example is if it is behind a paywall)
3. Verify that the source title and author matches the one in the article in which it was cited.
4. Review the source article and identify if the claim is supported by the article. (During this step, I used the Claim Verification rules where it applied)
5. Label each claim as supported, partially supported or not supported.
6. Record a remark explaining why you came to the verdict above.


For more details on how to properly verify claims, I'd recommend that you read this article Garcia, M. B. (2026, November 4). How Do You Know Whether a Paper Actually Supports the Claim It Is Cited For? In Part 02: Finding What the  Literature Really Says. The Researcher's Field Guide. https://manuelgarcia.info/guides/research/guide/paper-supports-cited-claim
